# Daily Discovery: Church of Santa María (Lebeña)
**Country:** España

The Church of Santa María (Lebeña) (Spanish: Iglesia de Santa María (Lebeña)) is a medieval building located in Cillorigo de Liébana, Spain. The architectural style is Pre-Romanesque.It is classed as a Bien de Interés Cultural and has been protected since 1893.List of Bien de Interés Cultural in Cantabria
