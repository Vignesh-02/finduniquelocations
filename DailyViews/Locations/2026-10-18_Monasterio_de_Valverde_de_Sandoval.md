# Daily Discovery: Monasterio de Valverde de Sandoval
**Country:** España

Santa María de Sandoval was a Roman Catholic monastery of the Cistercian Order in Spain from the 12th century until 1835. The oldest part was built in the Romanesque style, but later additions are Gothic. Today its ruins lie in Villaverde de Sandoval. It has been listed as a monumento histórico-artístico (historical artistic monument) since 3 June 1931. In 2015, the Spanish government listed it as an "associated element" of the Camino de Santiago, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
