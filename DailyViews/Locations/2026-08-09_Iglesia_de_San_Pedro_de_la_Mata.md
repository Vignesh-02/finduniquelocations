# Daily Discovery: Iglesia de San Pedro de la Mata
**Country:** España

San Pedro de la Mata is a ruined church located in the middle of the countryside, at about 3 km southwest of the village (pedanía) of Casalgordo,(see note1) in the municipality of Sonseca (province of Toledo, Castile-La Mancha, Spain).The original foundations of a church, a granite platform, can be clearly seen. Some fragments of walls and a couple of arches also remain.
