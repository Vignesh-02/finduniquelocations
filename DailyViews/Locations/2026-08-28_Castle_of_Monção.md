# Daily Discovery: Castle of Monção
**Country:** Portugal

The Castle of Monção (Portuguese: Castelo de Monção) is a medieval castle in the civil parish of Monção e Troviscoso, municipality of Monção, the Portuguese district of Viana do Castelo.It is classified as a National Monument.
