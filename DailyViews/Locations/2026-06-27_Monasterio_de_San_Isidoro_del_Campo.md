# Daily Discovery: Monasterio de San Isidoro del Campo
**Country:** España

The Monastery of San Isidoro del Campo (Spanish: monasterio de San Isidoro del Campo) is a monastery located in Santiponce, Spain. It was declared Bien de Interés Cultural in 1872.The monastery was founded in 1301 by Alonso Pérez de Guzmán.The interior houses an altarpiece by Juan Martínez Montañes.List of Bien de Interés Cultural in the Province of Seville
