# Daily Discovery: convent of las Carmelitas de San José
**Country:** España

The Convent of las Carmelitas de San José (Spanish: Convento de las Carmelitas de San José) is a Carmelite convent located in Guadalajara, Spain. It was declared Bien de Interés Cultural in 1992.Media related to Convent of the Carmelitas of San José, Guadalajara at Wikimedia Commons
