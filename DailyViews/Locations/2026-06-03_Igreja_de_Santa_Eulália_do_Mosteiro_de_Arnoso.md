# Daily Discovery: Igreja de Santa Eulália do Mosteiro de Arnoso
**Country:** Portugal

Church of Santa Eulalia of the Monastery of Arnoso (Portuguese: Igreja de Santa Eulália do Mosteiro de Arnoso) is a church in Vila Nova de Famalicão, Portugal. It was classified as a National Monument in 1938.The original church was founded in the 7th century on the initiative of San Frutuoso, Bishop of Dume and Braga during the Visigoth era, and destroyed by the Moors in the 11th century. It was later rebuilt by King García II of Galicia.
